# Investment Decision Framework

## Decision Goal

Support property investment decision-making by systematically comparing London boroughs across multiple investment criteria.

### What this system does

* Compares London boroughs using multiple sources of evidence.
* Provides transparent and explainable reasoning behind each evaluation.
* Supports investor decision-making rather than replacing human judgement.

## Decision Criteria

The following criteria represent the primary dimensions used to evaluate the investment attractiveness of London boroughs.

| Decision Criterion | Why it matters |
|--------------------|----------------|
| Financial Accessibility | Determines whether entering the market is financially realistic. |
| Return Potential | Evaluates the investment's potential to generate rental income and long-term capital appreciation. |
| Accessibility | Measures how well the location connects residents to employment, services, and transport networks. |
| Market Demand | Evaluates whether sustained buyer and tenant demand supports long-term investment performance. |
